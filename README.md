## What could your team make?

Choose a question you can explore at a manageable scale. Your project could be:

* A small quantum or hybrid solution to a scheduling, routing, allocation, or other application problem.
* A comparison of quantum and classical approaches, with results and limitations explained.
* A tool, visualizer, or teaching aid that helps a specific audience.
* A supported analysis of where quantum computing fits a problem—and where it does not.

Need an idea? Consider emergency resource distribution, hospital scheduling, energy, agriculture, cybersecurity, finance, or another subject you know or want to learn about.

You are not expected to prove quantum advantage. Explain the problem, show what you tried, and be clear about what the evidence supports. See the handbook's [Project Guide](PARTICIPANT_HANDBOOK.md#2-project-guide) for examples.

## Final submission

**Submit by November 6, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time.**

Complete both steps through [**Deepstation.ai**](https://deepstation.ai):

1. **Upload a project video no longer than 15 minutes**, including any demo.
2. **Submit your GitHub project repository link.**

The video should explain your motivation, problem, quantum approach, demo or analysis, results, limitations, and team contributions. The repository should include a README, relevant code or notebooks, setup instructions, analysis, and supporting results. Make sure judges can access both items.

**Deepstation.ai is the only platform for final submissions.** Creating a GitHub repository or opening a pull request does not complete your submission.

For detailed instructions, see [GitHub and Final Submission](PARTICIPANT_HANDBOOK.md#6-github-and-final-submission) in the handbook.

## Closing ceremony and participation certificates

Each team must prepare a **10-minute sharing presentation, including a demo**, for the November 6 presentation session from **1:30–3:00 p.m.**

This is separate from the submitted video of up to 15 minutes. The sharing presentation **does not affect final scores**. Attendance and participation in this session are required to receive a **participation certificate**.
